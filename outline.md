# Outline — The Pure Faith Crusade

## Structure

- **Total chapters:** 15
- **Target word count:** 75,000 (~5,000 words/chapter)
- **POV rotation:** Elena (primary, Ch 1,2,4,5,11,15), Sam (Ch 6,13), Voss (Ch 7), Maya (Ch 8,14), Hale (Ch 9), Ruth (Ch 10), Marcus (Ch 12)
- **Act breakdown:** Act I = Ch 1-4 (0-28%), Act II = Ch 5-11 (28-77%), Act III = Ch 12-15 (77-100%)
- **Timeline:** Ch 1-5 span 3 days; Ch 6-8 span 2 weeks; Ch 9-11 span 4 days; Ch 12-15 span 3 days

## Themes

1. **Faith vs. Doubt as literal force** — In a world where belief has
   physical power, what does it mean to doubt?
2. **The cost of survival** — Can you stay human when the world demands
   you become a weapon to survive?
3. **Maternal love vs. moral duty** — Elena must choose between saving
   her daughter and saving the world.
4. **The corruption of purity** — The Faith began with genuine intent
   to heal; it ends in fanaticism. Purity corrupted is the most
   dangerous corruption.
5. **Doubt as the last human faculty** — The ability to question, to
   be uncertain, to hold two ideas at once — this is what makes us
   human, and what the Faith destroys.
