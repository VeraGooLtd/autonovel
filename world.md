# World Bible — The Pure Faith Crusade

## Cosmology & History

### The Sundering Era (2020-2030)

The world remembers three great ruptures: the floods, the faith, and
the burning. Each is a layer. The floods came first.

**The Great Flooding (2023):** Sea-level rise and unprecedented rainfall
submerged the Thames Estuary and parts of East Anglia. Three million
displaced. The government's response — mass evacuation centers called
"Sanctuaries" — became the template for everything that followed.

**The Heat Winter (2024):** A disrupted Gulf Stream dumped Arctic ice
into the North Atlantic. Winter temperatures in the south dropped to
-18C while the north baked at 35C. Crop failures. Power grid
collapse. The military declared martial law in six counties.

**The Discovery (2026):** During flood-mitigation excavation in the
Cotswolds, workers uncovered crystalline formations that resonated
when groups of people prayed or meditated near them. Initial reports
called them "faithstones." The Ministry of Emergency Relief classified
the find.

**The Awakening (2027):** Cardinal Matthias Voss, then a minor
cleric in the revived Catholic hierarchy that had merged with emergency
services, conducted a ritual at the Cotswold site with 300 refugees.
The stones responded. Conviction arced through the crowd like lightning
made of meaning. Three people healed of conditions that had lasted
years. Twelve reported visions. The rest forgot their own names for
three days.

**The Crusade Declared (2028):** Voss published "The Gospel of
Conviction" -- the claim that the stones were divine gifts, that
faith now had literal power, and that the Faithless (those whose
belief could not generate Conviction) were obstacles to humanity's
salvation. The south declared independence under the Pure Faith.
The Sanctuary Network, originally a government evacuation program,
went underground.

**The Great Sanctuary Fire (2029):** Coordinated strikes on 47
Sanctuary sites across the south. Estimated 12,000 Faithless killed.
The North declared neutrality and closed its borders. Scotland and
Wales formally recognized the Northern Compact.

**The Conviction Pogroms (2029-2030):** Systematic identification and
"cleansing" of Conviction-less individuals. The Faith established
"Certainty Schools" to harvest children -- the demographic most
capable of generating strong Conviction. Those who resisted were
executed; those who converted were absorbed into the Crusade.

Now, in 2030, the south burns with faith that heals and kills in the
same breath. The Faithless hide in the ruins. The stones sing.

### The Deeper History (pre-Sundering)

The Faithstones are not new. They are older than Britain. Geological
evidence suggests they formed 65 million years ago during the Paleocene
thermal maximum, when a meteor impact in what is now Somerset created
a ring of crystalline pressure structures. The stones are not magnetic,
not piezoelectric -- they resonate at frequencies that align with
human neural oscillation patterns during peak emotional and spiritual
states.

The Romans noted "healing springs" at several sites now known to contain
stones. Celtic druids worshipped at the largest stone circle (now
Avebury, where seven stones form the Greater Circuit). Anglo-Saxon
chronicles record "miracle fields" that were abandoned after the
plague years. Each era's people built something over the stones and
then forgot, or were forgotten.

The stones do not create Conviction. They amplify it. They are
conductors, not sources. The energy comes from human belief -- but
only belief in something true about the believer's own experience.
Selfless love, genuine remorse, authentic courage, real sacrifice.
Delusions and self-deception produce only heat and noise.

### Iceberg Details (unexplained but hinted)

- The stones are slowly dying. Each major use cracks them further.
  The original Cotswold Circle of twelve stones has lost three to
  spontaneous fracture. At the current rate, they will all be
  inert within ten years.
- Children under 14 generate Conviction more easily but burn out
  faster. The Faith's leadership knows this and hides the casualties.
- Some Faithless are born immune to Conviction entirely -- they
  cannot feel its effects, generate it, or be affected by it. The
  Faith calls them "Nulls" and hunts them. Elena is one, though she
  does not know it yet.
- The stones resonate in a network. Using one strengthens all others.
  This is why the Faith expanded: each new stone made the others more
  powerful, until the entire south hummed with barely-contained faith.

## Magic System

### Conviction (Hard Rules -- Tonal Law)

Conviction is the literal manifestation of faith. When a believer
holds a true conviction -- a belief grounded in their genuine experience
and paid for with real sacrifice -- and that belief is witnessed by
others who share or acknowledge its truth, reality bends to accommodate
it.

The mechanics:

1. **The Truth Clause**: Conviction only works when the believer's
   core belief is TRUE about themselves. "I am afraid" works. "I am
   brave" works only if the believer has genuinely been brave. Deliberate
   self-deception produces Conviction backlash -- psychic burns that
   manifest as physical scars.

2. **The Sacrifice Clause**: To channel Conviction, the caster must
   sacrifice something they genuinely value: a memory (forgetting a
   loved one's face), a skill (losing the ability to play guitar),
   a physical capability (sight, a hand, the use of a limb), a
   relationship (the target forgets the caster ever existed), or
   life itself. The greater the Conviction, the greater the price.
   The sacrifice is permanent -- there is no refunding Conviction.

3. **The Witness Clause**: Conviction requires witnesses. A single
   believer generates only enough force for minor effects (healing a
   cut, briefly warming cold metal). A crowd of hundreds can level
   buildings. The witnesses must be present and must either believe
   (amplifying) or witness without disbelief (stabilizing). Active
   disbelief unravels Conviction entirely.

4. **The Transformation Clause**: Conviction cannot create from
   nothing. It can heal, protect, strengthen, inspire, and reshape
   existing matter, but it cannot raise the truly dead, create
   matter ex nihilo, or violate conservation of energy. Healing
   requires existing biological structure to work with. Resurrection
   can only delay the crossing -- a healed corpse returns briefly,
   but the debt compounds until death claims it properly.

5. **The Corrosion Clause**: Every use of Conviction erodes the
   caster's capacity for doubt. Doubt is the source of empathy,
   critical thinking, and the ability to see nuance. As doubt fades,
   the caster becomes more certain, more zealous, less human. Full
   erosion produces a "Crusader" -- a being of pure conviction who
   cannot understand those who doubt and sees their uncertainty as
   a disease to be cured.

6. **The Stone Clause**: Faithstones amplify Conviction by a factor
   of 10-100 depending on proximity and the stone's condition. The
   amplification also amplifies the Corrosion effect. Regular use of
   stones accelerates the caster toward Crusader-hood. This is by
   design -- the Faith wants Crusaders.

7. **The Feedback Loop**: When multiple Conviction-users operate in
   the same area, their convictions resonate. Shared faith amplifies
   everyone's power multiplicatively. But shared delusion amplifies
   backlash. This is why Crusader squads operate in packs.

8. **The Cost Manifestation**: Conviction costs are paid at the
   moment of casting, not after. The caster feels the sacrifice
   happening as they channel the energy. This makes Conviction use
   agonizing, not ecstatic. The pain is part of the fuel.

### Soft Magic (Elena's Doubt)

Elena does not understand Conviction intellectually. She encounters
it as a force of nature -- something that hums in the air near
converted sites, that makes her teeth ache, that makes Faithless
children cry for no reason. She knows it is dangerous, that it
burns away doubt and leaves people hollow-eyed and smiling. She
does not know she is immune.

Elena's relationship with Conviction is tactile, sensory, emotional.
She feels its absence as a kind of pressure, like the moment before
lightning strikes. She understands it in fragments -- a half-remembered
childhood encounter with a street preacher, her daughter's sudden
fascination with Faith recruiters, the way the Sanctuary's oldest
survivors flinch when someone mentions "healing."

Her ignorance is a shield. Because she does not believe in Conviction's
divine origin, the Faith cannot use her as a witness to amplify their
power. Because she has never generated Conviction, she cannot be
corroded by it.

### Societal Implications

**Governance**: The Pure Faith operates as a theodemocracy. Conviction-
wrought citizens serve as judges, healers, and soldiers. Their word is
law because their belief is unshakeable. The Faithless are governed
through a parallel underground system of mutual aid and survival.
The Northern Compact maintains secular law but has begun experimenting
with Conviction-nullification techniques.

**Commerce**: The Faith traded gold-backed currency for "Faith Credits"
-- measured Conviction contributions accepted as payment. A healing
costs 500 credits. Protection from crusaders costs 2000. A night's
shelter in a Sanctuary costs 50. The Faithless use a barter economy:
information, skills, and pre-Sundering tech. The rarest commodity is
unbroken books -- the Faith burns libraries they cannot control.

**Education**: Faith runs "Certainty Schools" where children are
trained from age 5 to generate Conviction on command. Curriculum
includes theology, sacrifice economics, and "The Art of True Belief."
The Faithless run secret schools called "Doubt Houses" where children
learn to question everything and hide their thoughts. Literacy rates
among Faithless children are higher but knowledge is fragmented.

**Class Structure**: The Faith has three tiers: the Conviction-Wrought
(elite, cannot doubt, serve the Faith directly), the Faithful (generate
Conviction but retain doubt, serve as witnesses and soldiers), and the
Faithless (cannot generate Conviction, treated as pariahs). The
Northern Compact has two tiers: the Freeborn (born in the Compact) and
the Refugees (Faithless who crossed the border).

**Crime**: Faith crimes include "Belief Violence" (using Conviction to
harm), "False Conviction" (self-deception that causes backlash), and
"Witness Abandonment" (failing to testify when Conviction is used
righteously). Faithless crime is simpler: survival theft, information
trading, and "stone-running" (stealing Faithstones for the resistance).
The most dangerous criminal is the "Conviction-Null" -- someone born
without the ability to generate or resist Conviction. The Faith offer
bounties for Nulls.

**Family Life**: Faith families are organized around "Conviction
Purity" -- bloodlines that generate strong Conviction are bred
together. Children are tested at age 5. Faithless children are
"cleansed" -- either converted or exiled. Faith children are taught
that doubt is a spiritual disease. Faithless families form through
choice -- "Sanctuary Bonds" where adults swear to protect each other's
doubt.

**Childhood**: Faith children grow up in communal dormitories, trained
to believe in unison. Faithless children grow up in hiding, taught to
read body language and identify Faith patrols before they arrive.
Both are starved of normal childhood -- Faith children have no
imagination (doubt is suppressed), Faithless children have no safety
(love makes you vulnerable to Conviction detection).

**Disability**: Conviction cannot heal congenital conditions -- it can
only accelerate natural healing of acquired injuries. Those born
disabled are either "blessed by God's plan" (Faith view) or "untouchable"
(Faithless view). Some Faithless have developed technology to compensate
-- mechanical prosthetics, hearing aids, etc. The Faith sees this as
hubris.

**Aging**: Conviction slows aging but does not stop it. Long-term
Conviction users become gaunt and luminous-eyed, their bodies preserved
but their minds narrowed to a single purpose. The oldest Faith wraiths
are 120 but look 40. They remember everything they have sacrificed --
including the faces of people they have loved and forgotten.

## Geography

### The Fractured Isles (2030)

The United Kingdom no longer exists as a unified state. The climate
disasters and the Faith's rise have split it into zones:

**New Jerusalem (South England, Faith-controlled):**
The "Conviction Heartland." Centered on the Cotswold stone circles
but extending from London (now "New Jerusalem City") south to the
English Channel. The Thames runs through the middle, crossed by
faith-blessed bridges that only the Conviction-wrought can cross
safely. The air here smells of incense and ozone. Stone dust
permeates everything -- ground from the Faithstones that the Faith
has broken to spread their power.

Key locations:
- New Jerusalem City (formerly London south bank): Population 80,000.
  The Faith's capital. The Cathedral of Conviction rises from the
  ruins of St. Paul's. Faith Credits are the only accepted currency.
- The Cotswold Circles: Seven concentric stone rings. Each ring adds
  10x amplification to the previous. The innermost ring (the "Altar
  of Awakening") can heal a hundred people at once.
- Avebury Major: The largest stone circle, pre-Cambrian. Contains
  seven stones forming the Greater Circuit. The Faith's high command
  meets here during the Conjunction (when all stones resonate).
- Faithstone Quarries: Where the Faith mines and shapes new stones.
  Conditions are deadly -- exposure to raw stone resonance causes
  "Faithmadness" (temporary delusions of divinity).

**The Shallows (London north bank and surrounding ruins):**
The Faithless heartland. A warren of underground tunnels, bombed-out
buildings, and improvised shelters. The Thames here is polluted,
crossed by rickety faith-neutral bridges (coated in substances that
block Conviction resonance). The air smells of smoke, wet concrete,
and the chemical markers the Faithless use to identify safe houses
(dried kelp, burnt sage, iron filings).

Key locations:
- Sanctuary Station (ruined St. Pancras): Elena's base. The old
  station's vaults provide natural shielding from Conviction. The
  Network's headquarters.
- The Warrens: A maze of subway tunnels beneath the City of London.
  Connected to Sanctuary Station. Home to 3,000 Faithless.
- Market Cross: An open-air bazaar where the Faithless trade in
  pre-Sundering artifacts. No Conviction works here -- the stones
  embedded in the foundation by the Network neutralize all faith.
- The Foundry: Where faith-neutralized tech is repaired. The
  Network's most guarded secret -- if the Faith learned they could
  build Conviction-dampening devices, they would take it.

**The Midlands Wastes:**
A buffer zone controlled by the Merchant Companies. Rolling hills
pockmarked with the ruins of small towns. No Faithstones, no strong
Conviction. The perfect neutral ground. The air here is neutral --
no incense, no ozone, just dust and the smell of growing things.
The Merchant Companies maintain a fragile peace by selling to both
sides and never taking sides.

Key locations:
- Birmingham Nexus: The largest settlement. A trading post built
  around a pre-Sundering factory complex.
- The Glass Roads: Paved with recycled glass from the Sundering
  era. Faith-neutral -- Conviction cannot take hold on glass.
- Threcker's Rest: A fortified caravanserai. The only place in the
  Midlands where Faithless can meet with Faith defectors openly
  (under the "Glass Truce" -- no Conviction within 100 meters of
  glass).

**The North (Northern Compact):**
Cold, scarred, neutral. The Gulf Stream disruption left the north
permanently colder. The Compact maintains order through military
discipline and technology. They have no Faithstones but have begun
studying Conviction-nullification. The air smells of woodsmoke and
frozen earth.

Key locations:
- New Hadrian's Wall: A technological barrier that blocks Conviction
  resonance. Built from faith-neutral materials.
- The Academy: Where the Compact studies pre-Sundering science.
  They are trying to understand Conviction without using it.
- Frosthold: The northernmost settlement. Population 5,000. The
  last place where traditional medicine (not Conviction-healing)
  is the norm.

**The Wastes (East Anglia, Wales, Scotland):**
Uninhabitable or sparsely populated. East Anglia is underwater.
Wales has declared independence and trades with the Compact. Scotland
has a separate government and refuses all Faith contact.

### Sensory Signatures

Each region has a distinct sensory signature:

- **New Jerusalem**: Incense (myrrh and frankincense), ozone, stone
  dust, the metallic taste of Conviction in the air, distant humming
  from the stones.
- **The Shallows**: Smoke (burnt plastic and wet wool), the chemical
  tang of faith-neutralizing compounds, iron (from blood and rust),
  the damp smell of underground.
- **The Midlands**: Dust, growing things (wild grass and weeds through
  rubble), woodsmoke, the oily smell of machinery.
- **The North**: Woodsmoke, frozen earth, the sharp smell of ice,
  the metallic tang of cold.
- **The Wastes**: Salt (from the floods), decay, the smell of things
  drowning or dying.

## Factions & Politics

### 1. The Pure Faith (Theocracy)

**Core belief**: Conviction is divine power. The Faithless are obstacles
to salvation. The Crusade will purify Britain.

**Power structure**: Cardinal Voss (Supreme Pontiff) -> Arch-Crusaders
(Conviction-wrought lieutenants) -> Faithful (Conviction-generating
soldiers) -> Faithless (slaves/converts).

**Agenda**: 1) Control all Faithstones. 2) Convert or eliminate all
Faithless. 3) Expand northward once the stones are unified.

**Internal conflict**: The Faith leadership knows the stones are dying
and that Conviction-corrosion is inevitable. They plan to transfer
their consciousness into Conviction-powered artifacts before they
burn out -- a twisted form of immortality.

**Capabilities**: Conviction-wrought healing, faith-shields,
probability manipulation (minor), mass inspiration. Limited by
geography (stones), witness requirements, and escalating costs.

### 2. The Sanctuary Network (Resistance)

**Core belief**: Doubt is strength. Faith without question is slavery.
Everyone deserves to choose their own truth.

**Power structure**: Decentralized. Elena coordinates through a
courier network. Regional commanders (Tomas, et al.) have autonomy.

**Agenda**: 1) Protect the Faithless. 2) Neutralize the Faith's
stones. 3) Expose the truth about Conviction to the Faithful.

**Internal conflict**: Elena wants to expose the truth about
Conviction; some Network members want to simply survive. The
"Purists" (led by Commander Hale) believe all Faith must be
destroyed, even the faithful who were born into it. Elena opposes
this, creating internal tension.

**Capabilities**: Faith-neutralization technology (developed by
the Foundry), underground knowledge networks, pre-Sundering military
records, and Elena's immunity to Conviction.

### 3. The Merchant Companies (Mercantile Neutrality)

**Core belief**: Profit from both sides. War feeds commerce.

**Power structure**: Guild-based. Each guild (Weapons, Information,
Technology, Medicine) sends representatives to the Merchants' Council.

**Agenda**: Maintain the Midlands as neutral ground. Sell to both
Faith and Faithless. Prevent either side from gaining a monopoly.

**Internal conflict**: Increasing pressure from both sides to choose.
The Faith demands exclusive trade agreements; the Network demands
free passage. The Companies' neutrality is eroding.

**Capabilities**: Technology from the Foundry, pre-Sundering weapons,
information networks, and the Glass Truce (neutral territory).

### 4. The Northern Compact (Secular Militarism)

**Core belief**: Reason over faith. Humanity survives through technology
and discipline, not superstition.

**Power structure**: Military junta. General Shaw (Supreme Commander)
-> Regional governors -> Community Councils.

**Agenda**: 1) Study Conviction to counter it. 2) Maintain independence.
3) Prepare for the Faith's northern advance.

**Internal conflict**: The Compact's scientists are making progress
on Conviction-nullification, but the process requires Conviction
subjects -- which means kidnapping Faith children. The moral cost
divides the leadership.

**Capabilities**: Pre-Sundering military tech, Conviction-nullification
research, disciplined troops, the Wall (Conviction-blocking barrier).

### 5. The Defectors (Internal Faith opposition)

**Core belief**: Conviction is real but was perverted. It can be
redeemed, not destroyed.

**Power structure**: Loose network. Father Marcus is the most prominent
defector and theologian. Others include "Sleeper" Faith soldiers who
haven't fully corroded yet and "The Weeping" -- Faith converts who
regained enough doubt to flee.

**Agenda**: 1) Reveal the truth about the stones. 2) Find a way to
reverse Conviction-corrosion. 3) Split the Faith from within.

**Internal conflict**: Some defectors want to destroy the stones
outright; others want to reclaim them. The Faith hunts them as
heretics twice over.

**Capabilities**: Inside knowledge of Faith doctrine and Conviction
mechanics, access to Faith infrastructure, and the credibility of
former believers.

### Political Tensions

- The Faith is running out of stones and growing desperate. Their
  "Project Conviction" -- mass conversion of the Faithless -- is
  accelerated because the stones are dying faster than expected.
- The Network's Foundry has developed a Conviction-dampener, but it
  requires rare earth elements found only in the Wastes (underwater
  East Anglia). An expedition would have to go through Faith territory.
- The Compact's experiments on Faith children are creating a new
  problem: "Null-born" children who are immune to Conviction but
  also unable to generate it. The Faith wants to kill them; the
  Network wants to protect them.
- The Merchant Companies are being squeezed -- both sides demand
  exclusive deals. If they fracture, the Midlands become a war zone.

## Natural World

### Flora

Most pre-Sundering plant life is dead or mutated. The Faithstones
themselves are living in a sense -- they grow slowly, extending
crystalline structures over decades. Faith-affected plants grow in
geometric patterns near stones. Faithless areas have weeds that
thrive on neglect: thistle, dock, and a mutant strain of nettles
that sting with a paralyzing venom (the Network uses these as
boundary markers).

The one plant that thrives everywhere is "conviction-grass" -- a
hardy weed that grows in cracks of ruined concrete. It bends toward
areas of strong Conviction but withers where doubt is high. The
Faithless use it as an early-warning system.

### Fauna

Most large fauna died in the Sundering floods and fires. What remains:
rats (mutated, smarter, immune to Conviction -- they swarm Faith sites),
crows (carry information across distances, used by both sides as
messengers), feral dogs (Faithless companions, trained to detect
Conviction-dampening compounds), and a species of spider that weaves
faith-neutral silk (the Network harvests this for their dampeners).

Mutated "stone-rats" in the Cotswolds have developed a symbiotic
relationship with Faithstones -- they eat the crystal dust and excrete
Conviction-amplifying compounds. The Faith harvests these as a
performance-enhancing drug ("stone-rat powder"). Addiction causes
rapid Conviction-corrosion.

## Cultural Details

### Faith Culture

**Religion**: The Pure Faith blends Catholic liturgy with Conviction
rituals. Mass is performed at stone circles. The Eucharist is real
bread blessed with Conviction (which makes it sustaining for weeks).
Confession requires witnessing -- a priest can only absolve sins
witnessed by another believer.

**Festivals**: 
- The Conjunction (once per year, when all stones resonate): A
  week-long festival of mass Conviction channeling. The Faith
  leadership draws power from thousands of believers at once.
- Conviction Day (anniversary of the Awakening): Mass conversions,
  public "healings," and the Cleansing (identification of Faithless).

**Food**: Faith bread (Conviction-blessed, never spoils, sustains
for days). Stone-fruit (grown near stones, tastes of copper).
Protein blocks (lab-grown, distributed by the Faith). The Faithless
eat whatever they can scavenge, supplemented by Network-grown mushrooms
and the conviction-grass roots (bitter, but filling).

**Clothing**: Faith wear white robes with bronze crosses (the cross
is a stylized stone circuit). Crusaders wear armor infused with
stone dust (makes it Conviction-resistant). Faithless wear muted
colors, practical clothes, and cover their faces with cloth to hide
from Faith surveillance. Doubt sigils (question marks) mark safe
houses.

**Language**: Faith-speak includes Conviction terminology: "Witness
me," "Bear true," "Share the burden," "Let it burn clean." Faithless
use coded language: "The stones sing" (danger near), "The glass
holds" (safe house), "The children sleep" (children are hidden).

### Faithless Culture

**Coming of Age**: Faithless children undergo "The Naming" at age 12.
They choose an alias and swear a "Sanctuary Bond" with two other
children -- a loyalty that supersedes family. Breaking the bond is
the worst crime in the Faithless world.

**Art**: Faithless art is forbidden by the Faith but thrives in
underground spaces. Murals in the Warrens depict "false saints" --
people who resisted the Faith. Music is quiet, acoustic, passed
by memory. Songs encode safe routes and warning codes.

**Death**: The Faithless burn their dead (ashes scattered in the
Thames). They refuse Conviction-healing because it requires faith
in the stones. Death is final and accepted.

### Customs and Taboos

**Faith taboos**: Questioning doctrine, touching a dead stone,
speaking the True Name of Conviction (which would reveal its
non-divine origin), refusing to witness a Conviction working.

**Faithless taboos**: Speaking your real name aloud, trusting a
defector, using Conviction for healing (seen as spiritual corruption),
touching a Faithstone directly (the resonance burns Faithless more
intensely).

## Internal Consistency Rules

1. Conviction always costs the caster something permanent before the
   effect manifests. Never after. Never a small price for a large
   effect.
2. Every Conviction effect requires at least one witness who has not
   rejected faith. A lone Faithless person is immune.
3. Faithstones are depleting. The Faith's power is declining. Desperation
   drives their escalation.
4. Conviction-corrosion is progressive and irreversible. A Crusader
   cannot recover their doubt. They can only spread it.
5. No new Conviction abilities can appear in Act III without
   foreshadowing in Act I.
6. Faith-neutralizing technology exists but is rare and fragile. The
   Foundry is the only producer.
7. Children under 14 are the most powerful Conviction sources but
   burn out fastest. The Faith knows this. The Network is learning it.
8. Glass and iron disrupt Conviction resonance. This is the basis
   of the Glass Truce and the Network's dampeners.
9. Conviction cannot heal congenital conditions. Only acquired
   injuries and diseases. This is a deliberate limitation.
10. The "True Name" of Conviction (if spoken aloud by a believer)
    causes all nearby Conviction to collapse. Only Elena knows the
    True Name, without knowing she knows it.
