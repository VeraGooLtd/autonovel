Crusades return to UK in 2030: The Pure Faith Crusade

In 2030, after climate disasters and economic collapse fracture Britain, a
fundamentalist Christian movement called the Pure Faith seizes control of
the south and declares a new "Crusade" against secular society. The story
follows Elena, a former civil servant who runs an underground Sanctuary
Network, as she navigates a world where faith has become a weapon and the
line between resistance and fanaticism is razor-thin.
