# Characters

## Maya Vance (Non-POV, revealed in Ch 8)

- **Role:** Elena's daughter, raised in the Faith, Faithful-level Conviction generator
- **Age:** 16 (born 2014). Was 8 when "death" occurred in 2026.
- **Speech pattern:** Trained Faith-speak ("By the stones, Mother"), but with
  slips of doubt when stressed. Formal, then urgent when conflicted.
- **Arc:** Indoctrinated Faithful -> Awakened Null -> Free agent (Ch 8-15)
- **Secret:** She is a Conviction Null born — her brain cannot generate
  or be affected by Conviction. The Faith enhanced her body with
  conviction-tech to make her a weapon anyway. This is why she
  survived her "death" — she was taken, not killed.
- **Relationships:**
  - Elena: daughter who thinks her mother abandoned her (Act 1);
    discovers Elena is her mother (Act 2); chooses to help Elena
    destroy the stones even though it removes her enhancements (Act 3)
  - Cardinal Voss: "Father" figure, handler, believes Maya is the
    key to renewing the stones
  - Young Sam: Maya protects him because he resembles her younger self

### Wound/Want/Need/Lie

- **Ghost:** At age 8, Maya ran toward Faith recruiters after Elena
  said "no" to her questions about the Faith. Maya believes Elena
  rejected her faith — and by extension, rejected her.
- **Wound:** Chronic abandonment; believes love requires conversion.
  She cannot trust anyone who doesn't share her beliefs.
- **Lie:** "If you're not with the Faith, you're against the saved.
  Doubt is a luxury the dead can afford."
- **Want:** To prove herself worthy of the Faith's approval and
  become a true Crusader.
- **Need:** To accept that love doesn't require conversion. Her
  Null-status means she can never truly be Faith -- but that doesn't
  make her unworthy.
- **Arc:** Negative (Lie -> deeper Lie -> breakthrough -> Truth)
  Maya starts in the Lie (faith = worth), doubles down through Act 2,
  has her crisis at the Midpoint revelation, and chooses Truth (she
  doesn't need faith to be worthy) at the climax.

### Three-Slider Profile

- **Proactivity:** 8/10 — She is the Faith's weapon, actively deployed
- **Likability:** 4/10 — Indoctrinated, can be cold, but moments of
  doubt make her sympathetic
- **Competence:** 9/10 — Enhanced by conviction-tech, peak physical
  and tactical abilities

### Foreshadowing Planted

- Ch 3: Elena mentions Maya's "prayer beads" — Maya's beads are
  actually a conviction-tech tracking device (payoff: Ch 8)
- Ch 5: A Faith soldier mentions "the Null-child project" — Maya
  doesn't react but her hands shake (payoff: Ch 12)
- Ch 8: Maya sees Elena's face on a wanted poster and doesn't
  recognize her (payoff: Ch 9)

## Elena Vance (POV, primary protagonist)

- **Role:** Leader of the Sanctuary Network, former civil servant
- **Age:** 38 (born 1986). Age 30 when the Sundering began.
- **Speech pattern:** Bureaucratic shorthand from her pre-Sundering
  civil service career, blunt, finishes half-sentences. Uses Network
  codes ("glass holds," "stones sing"). Rarely expresses hope
  aloud. Drops formal address with people she trusts.
- **Arc:** Cynical survivor -> Reluctant leader -> Trusting community
  builder (Ch 1-15)
- **Secret:** She is a Conviction Null — born immune to Conviction's
  effects. Her pregnancy with Maya was the first time her immunity
  manifested (Maya's Null-status passed through Elena). She doesn't
  know this yet.
- **Relationships:**
  - Maya: daughter she believes died; mother who said "no" to faith (Act 1);
    discovers Maya is alive (Act 2); must choose between revenge/duty
    and motherhood (Act 3)
  - Tomas: veteran lieutenant, trusted brother-figure (Act 1);
    betrayed by her caution (Act 2); reconciled through shared sacrifice (Act 3)
  - Marcus: theological ally (Act 2-3); represents the faith Elena lost
  - Voss: unknowing father of the Faith that killed her daughter;
    she doesn't know he's her intellectual equal

### Wound/Want/Need/Lie

- **Ghost:** At age 8, Maya ran toward Faith recruiters after Elena
  said "no" to her questions about the Faith. Maya was taken. Elena
  believes Maya died in the subsequent purge. Elena witnessed the
  burning and never forgave herself for saying "no."
- **Wound:** Chronic survivor guilt; believes attachment is weakness.
  She keeps people at arm's length to protect them from her losses.
- **Lie:** "Trusting anyone gets them killed. Better to be alone.
  The dead don't bleed."
- **Want:** To survive and keep the remaining Faithless safe through
  isolation and secrecy. On the surface: destroy the Faith.
  Underneath: avenge Maya's death.
- **Need:** To trust in community — to believe that collective action
  matters more than individual survival. To forgive herself for
  Maya's "death" (the real one: choosing bureaucracy over her
  daughter's curiosity).
- **Arc:** Positive (Lie -> Truth). At the climax, Elena must choose
  between solo revenge (killing Voss, destroying the stones alone)
  and trusting the Network to execute a risky plan that could save
  Maya but might fail and kill everyone.

### Three-Slider Profile

- **Proactivity:** 9/10 — She actively runs the Network, makes hard
  choices, never waits for rescue
- **Likability:** 6/10 — Competent and principled, but cold,
  distrustful, morally flexible when survival demands it
- **Competence:** 9/10 — Strategic mind, tactical awareness, understands
  both Faith and Faithless systems

### Foreshadowing Planted

- Ch 1: Elena's beads never register on the Network's Conviction
  scanners (payoff: Ch 12)
- Ch 1: Elena tells a recruit "doubt is your shield" — she doesn't
  know why she's so sure (payoff: Ch 12)
- Ch 3: Elena can read Faith-encrypted communiques without decryption
  aids (payoff: Ch 12)
- Ch 6: Elena is unaffected by a crusader's Conviction burst (payoff: Ch 12)
- Ch 12: Young Sam's blood works with Elena's blood to neutralize
  a Faithstone (payoff: Ch 14, Ch 15)

## Marcus Thorne (POV, secondary protagonist)

- **Role:** Defrocked Faith priest, theologian, Elena's ally
- **Age:** 45 (born 1979). Was a rising star in the Catholic
  hierarchy before the Sundering.
- **Speech pattern:** Theological references, quotes scripture
  (both biblical and Faith doctrine). Second-guesses his own words.
  Speaks in parables when stressed. Uses formal address until he
  trusts someone.
- **Arc:** Guilty architect -> Reluctant defector -> Redeemer (Ch 4-15)
- **Secret:** He co-authored the Conviction Ritual with Cardinal Voss.
  He was the one who suggested using refugees as the initial
  "witness pool" because their desperation would generate stronger
  belief. He believes he damned thousands.
- **Relationships:**
  - Voss: Former closest friend and collaborator (Act 1-2);
    becomes his enemy when Marcus defects (Act 2); faces him
    at the climax (Act 3)
  - Elena: The theological conscience she lacks (Act 2-3);
    represents the faith Elena lost; she trusts him despite
    everything
  - Maya: Sees her as proof that Conviction corrupts children;
    his crisis of faith centers on whether he can save her

### Wound/Want/Need/Lie

- **Ghost:** He conducted the ritual that awakened Conviction,
  believing he was channeling divine power. When he realized it
  was the stones, not God, he tried to stop the Faith's expansion.
- **Wound:** Intense survivor guilt. He saved himself by defecting,
   but thousands died because of his work.
- **Lie:** "Knowledge without power is meaningless. I have the
   knowledge now, but I need power to undo what I've done."
- **Want:** To atone for creating Conviction by destroying it.
- **Need:** To accept that some things cannot be undone, only
  redeemed. To trust that his knowledge, shared with others,
  is enough.
- **Arc:** Negative-to-Positive. Starts in the Lie (needs power to
  fix his mistake), doubles down through Act 2 (tries to seize
  a Faithstone), has his crisis at the midpoint, and chooses Truth
  (working with others, not alone) at the climax.

### Three-Slider Profile

- **Proactivity:** 7/10 — Driven by guilt, but often hesitant,
  second-guessing
- **Likability:** 8/10 — Genuinely caring, haunted, tries to
  minimize harm
- **Competence:** 9/10 — Deep theological and Conviction knowledge,
  understands the system's mechanics

### Foreshadowing Planted

- Ch 4: Marcus mentions "the ritual required desperation, not devotion"
  (payoff: Ch 12)
- Ch 5: Marcus flinches when a child mentions "the man in black"
  (payoff: Ch 8 — it's Voss, who recruited Maya)
- Ch 9: Marcus's blood reacts to Elena's beads (payoff: Ch 12 —
  both are Null-adjacent)

## Cardinal Matthias Voss (Antagonist, POV in Ch 7)

- **Role:** Supreme Pontiff of the Pure Faith, architect of the Crusade
- **Age:** 52 (born 1971). Was a rising Catholic cardinal before the
  Sundering.
- **Speech pattern:** Pastoral warmth that curdles into command.
  Speaks in Faith doctrine, uses "the stones have spoken" as a
  rhetorical device. Quotes scripture to justify violence. Drops
  the pastoral tone when his authority is challenged.
- **Arc:** Zealous reformer -> Desperate maintainer -> Fanatic (Ch 3-15)
- **Secret:** He knows the stones are dying and has been hiding it.
  His "Project Conviction" is a last-ditch effort to create a new
  Faithstone through mass belief, which requires the sacrifice of
  the most powerful Conviction-generators — children like Maya.
- **Relationships:**
  - Marcus: Former best friend and collaborator; their betrayal
    destroyed their friendship (Act 1-2); faces him at the climax
    (Act 3)
  - Maya: Sees her as the key to renewing the stones; genuinely
    cares for her but treats her as a tool (Act 2-3)
  - Elena: Knows her only as a Faithless resistance leader;
    underestimates her as a Null

### Wound/Want/Need/Lie

- **Ghost:** Lost his faith in humanity during the Sundering
  floods. Watched children die in the evacuation centers because
  the system was too slow, too bureaucratic. Concluded that
  only absolute certainty can save people.
- **Wound:** Deep-seated belief that compassion without power
  is complicity. He cannot see a path to salvation that doesn't
  involve control.
- **Lie:** "Salvation requires sacrifice. If I must burn a few
  thousand souls to save millions, that is not cruelty — it is
   wisdom."
- **Want:** To save Britain by imposing absolute faith and order.
- **Need:** To accept that salvation cannot be forced. That love
  without freedom is slavery. That doubt is not weakness but
  the space where genuine choice lives.
- **Arc:** Flat arc (negative). Voss does not change. He doubles
  down on his Lie throughout. He is the immovable obstacle
  Elena must surpass, not convert.

### Three-Slider Profile

- **Proactivity:** 9/10 — Mastermind, orchestrator, always
  several moves ahead
- **Likability:** 3/10 — Charismatic when needed, but his methods
   are ruthless; he knows the cost and accepts it
- **Competence:** 10/10 — Theological master, strategist, understands
   Conviction mechanics better than anyone alive

### Foreshadowing Planted

- Ch 3: Voss mentions "the stones grow hungry" (payoff: Ch 10 —
  the stones are literally depleting)
- Ch 7: Voss tells Maya "you are the bridge between the old world
  and the new" (payoff: Ch 12 — she's the Null-child key)
- Ch 10: Voss's hands shake when he channels Conviction (payoff:
  Ch 13 — he's approaching Crusader-hood)

## Tomas Kael (POV, supporting protagonist)

- **Role:** Elena's veteran lieutenant, tactical commander of the Network
- **Age:** 51 (born 1970). Former military, joined the Network after
   his son joined the Faith and killed a Faithless family.
- **Speech pattern:** Military precision, calls everyone "soldier."
  Rarely speaks more than necessary. Uses tactical shorthand.
  Becomes more verbose when discussing his son.
- **Arc:** Vengeful survivor -> Loyal protector -> Sacrificial leader (Ch 2-15)
- **Secret:** He has been secretly communicating with his son (a
  Faith soldier) through coded messages hidden in supply runs.
  He hopes to bring the boy home but won't admit it even to Elena.
- **Relationships:**
  - Elena: Sworn brother, trusts her completely (Act 1);
    betrayed when she won't authorize a rescue mission (Act 2);
    reconciled when they fight side by side (Act 3)
  - His son: Absent presence; Tomas's motivation throughout
  - Maya: Initially sees her as an enemy; comes to see her as
    innocent (Act 2-3)

### Wound/Want/Need/Lie

- **Ghost:** His son joined the Faith at 16 and returned home
  different — he killed a Faithless family during his "cleansing."
   Tomas watched his son become a stranger.
- **Wound:** Deep paternal grief mixed with shame — he raised a
  killer. He projects this onto all Faith members.
- **Lie:** "The faithful are beyond saving. They deserve what
   they get. Mercy is the weapon they use against you."
- **Want:** To see his son killed in the war — to have justice.
  On the surface: destroy the Faith.
- **Need:** To forgive his son (and by extension, all the faithful)
  — to understand that people can change.
- **Arc:** Positive. Starts in the Lie (faithful are irredeemable),
  confronts it when Maya reminds him of his son (Act 2), and
  chooses Truth (protecting the defecting Faith soldiers) at the
  climax.

### Three-Slider Profile

- **Proactivity:** 8/10 — Decisive in combat, takes initiative
  in the field
- **Likability:** 7/10 — Gruff but honorable, protects his people
  fiercely
- **Competence:** 9/10 — Military expertise, tactical brilliance,
   deep knowledge of both sides' capabilities

### Foreshadowing Planted

- Ch 2: Tomas carries his son's childhood toy (a wooden soldier) —
  he never says why (payoff: Ch 11)
- Ch 6: Tomas refuses to kill a captured Faith soldier — "I know
  what they do, but I won't become them" (payoff: Ch 13)
- Ch 11: Tomas reads a coded message and weeps (payoff: Ch 14 —
  his son is dead, killed by Voss's orders)

## Commander Hale (Antagonist, internal)

- **Role:** Network commander, Elena's ideological opposite
- **Age:** 43 (born 1977). Pre-Sundering intelligence officer.
- **Speech pattern:** Pragmatic, ruthless, believes in efficiency
  over mercy. Speaks in cost-benefit analysis. No religious
  references — he rejected faith entirely.
- **Arc:** Purist revolutionary -> Betrayer -> Sacrificed (Ch 3-12)
- **Secret:** He has been negotiating with the Northern Compact to
   betray Elena's operation in exchange for safe passage north for
   his own faction. He believes the Network is too soft to win.
- **Relationships:**
  - Elena: Ideological ally who becomes enemy (Act 2);
    his betrayal costs lives (Act 2); Elena must decide his fate
    after capturing him (Act 3)

### Wound/Want/Need/Lie

- **Ghost:** His family died in the Great Fire because Elena's
  "mercy protocol" let a Faith patrol escape. He blames Elena.
- **Wound:** Believes compassion is responsible for death. Wants
  to harden the Network against its own mercy.
- **Lie:** "Sentiment is a tactical error. Kill them all now."
- **Want:** To take control of the Network and make it ruthless
  enough to win.
- **Need:** To understand that ruthlessness without purpose is
  just brutality. That some lines can't be crossed.
- **Arc:** Negative arc (Truth -> Lie -> deeper Lie -> downfall).
  Hale doubles down on his Lie, betrays Elena, and pays the price.

### Three-Slider Profile

- **Proactivity:** 7/10 — Ambitious, takes initiative, willing
  to break rules
- **Likability:** 2/10 — Ruthless, cold, treats people as assets
- **Competence:** 8/10 — Tactical brilliance, intelligence training,
   strategic thinker

### Foreshadowing Planted

- Ch 3: Hale argues for "mercy killing" the captured Faith soldiers
  (payoff: Ch 12 — he betrays the Network to the Faith)
- Ch 7: Hale's radio code is "Mercury" — the Faith uses this for
  defector coordination (payoff: Ch 10 — he's been talking to them)
- Ch 12: Hale's last words: "You should have killed them when you
  had the chance" (payoff: Ch 13 — Elena learns mercy IS the weapon)

## Young Sam (POV in Ch 13)

- **Role:** 12-year-old Conviction Null, key to the resolution
- **Age:** 12 (born 2018). Born after the Sundering.
- **Speech pattern:** Direct, curious, asks uncomfortable questions.
  No theological vocabulary — he was raised Faithless. Speaks plainly
  about things adults avoid.
- **Arc:** Hidden asset -> Revealed key -> Sacrifice (Ch 6-15)
- **Secret:** He is one of fewer than 20 "True Nulls" in Britain —
   people born completely immune to Conviction AND capable of
   neutralizing it in others. The Faith hunts Nulls; the Network
   protects them.
- **Relationships:**
  - Elena: Sees her as a protector; trusts her instinctively (Act 2)
  - Maya: Protects him because he reminds her of her younger self (Act 3)

### Wound/Want/Need/Lie

- **Ghost:** His parents were killed in a Faith purge when he was 6.
   He survived because his Conviction-immunity made him invisible
   to Faith sensors.
- **Wound:** Abandonment and survivor guilt. He doesn't understand
   why he was spared when others weren't.
- **Lie:** "I don't belong anywhere. I'm a monster."
- **Want:** To find a place where he fits; to understand why he's
   different.
- **Need:** To accept his uniqueness as a gift, not a curse. To
  understand that his difference is exactly what the world needs.
- **Arc:** Positive. He starts lost and afraid, discovers his purpose,
  and sacrifices himself to help Elena destroy the stones.

### Three-Slider Profile

- **Proactivity:** 8/10 — Resourceful, takes initiative, never
  waits to be rescued
- **Likability:** 9/10 — Innocent, curious, naturally trusting
- **Competence:** 7/10 — Street-smart, understands Conviction
  intuitively, but young and inexperienced

### Foreshadowing Planted

- Ch 6: Sam's hand passes through a Conviction-shield without effect
  (payoff: Ch 13 — he's a Null)
- Ch 8: Sam doesn't flinch when Maya's Conviction-enhanced form
  appears (payoff: Ch 12)
- Ch 11: Sam says "the dark places sing to me" — he can sense
  Faithstones (payoff: Ch 13 — he leads them to the central stone)

## Sister Ruth (POV in Ch 10)

- **Role:** Faith defector, former Faith healer, Marcus's contact
- **Age:** 34 (born 1988). Pre-Sundering ICU nurse.
- **Speech pattern:** Medical terminology mixed with Faith doctrine.
  Speaks softly, apologetically. Uses "I'm sorry" as a habitual
  phrase. Shows signs of early Faithblindness (repetitive speech).
- **Arc:** Zealous healer -> Wounded defector -> Messenger (Ch 5-12)
- **Secret:** She was the one who performed the Conviction-healing
  on Maya's parents' memorial service. She doesn't know Maya is
  alive and that she's been sent to "reunite" her with Elena.
- **Relationships:**
  - Marcus: Her confessor and guide out of the Faith (Act 3-4)
  - Maya: Unknowingly serves the girl she tried to heal
  - Elena: Carries the message that saves her life (Act 3)

### Wound/Want/Need/Lie

- **Ghost:** She healed dozens of Faith soldiers using Conviction,
  not knowing each healing eroded her patients' humanity. She
  watched them become Crusaders.
- **Wound:** Medical guilt — she helped create the very monsters
  she now fights.
- **Lie:** "I was helping them. I didn't know what I was doing."
  (True at first, but becomes a shield against responsibility.)
- **Want:** To undo the harm she caused by finding a cure for
  Conviction-corrosion.
- **Need:** To accept that some harms cannot be undone, only
  witnessed and atoned for.
- **Arc:** Flat arc (her Truth is fixed — she must bear witness).
   She provides the information that enables Elena's final choice.

### Three-Slider Profile

- **Proactivity:** 5/10 — Mostly reactive, follows Marcus's lead
- **Likability:** 8/10 — Genuinely remorseful, tries to minimize harm
- **Competence:** 7/10 — Medical knowledge, Faith healing expertise,
  limited field awareness
